About Me

I am a Computer Science student at Sheridan College, an independent software developer, and an aspiring technology entrepreneur. I previously completed an Honours Bachelor of Science in Life Sciences with a Minor in Economics at McMaster University, providing me with an interdisciplinary foundation in scientific research, quantitative analysis, and problem-solving.

My technical interests include distributed systems, blockchain technology, cybersecurity, cloud computing, networking, and software engineering. I am particularly interested in developing practical solutions that combine emerging technologies with established engineering principles to address real-world challenges.

Beyond my academic coursework, I independently design and develop software projects involving decentralized infrastructure, secure access management, and interactive hardware-integrated applications. These projects reflect my interest in technology innovation, entrepreneurship, and the development of practical software solutions.

My current undergraduate research investigates statistical approaches to detecting abnormal resource usage in Kubernetes workloads. Through this research, I am exploring the intersection of cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, and quantitative analysis.

I am interested in opportunities involving software engineering, applied research, technology innovation, and entrepreneurial collaboration, while continuing to expand my technical expertise and develop solutions with meaningful real-world applications.
